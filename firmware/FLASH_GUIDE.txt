KINGROON KP3S MARLIN FIRMWARE V1 - FLASH GUIDE

1. Check the upgraded hotend and thermistor hardware before proceeding.
2. Build using BUILD_FIRMWARE.bat on Windows or BUILD_FIRMWARE.sh on Linux.
   The prompt requires typing 340 to acknowledge the high-temperature hardware.
3. Copy firmware_output\FLASH_KP3S\Robin_nano.bin to the root of a FAT32 microSD card.
4. Power the printer off.
5. Insert the card and power the printer on.
6. Do not interrupt power while the board is flashing.

After a successful flash the bootloader may rename the file to ROBIN_NANO.CUR.
HIGH-TEMPERATURE V1 NOTE
------------------------
This V1 build allows a 340 C hotend target and assumes a 100k B3950 350 C thermistor compatible with Marlin table 61 plus an all-metal hotend / heater / wiring rated for at least 350 C. Before heating, verify a plausible room-temperature reading. Do not use a stock table-1 thermistor or PTFE-lined hotend at 340 C. Run PID autotune after changing the hotend hardware.
